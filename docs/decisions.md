# MomentumDesk — Decisions Log

The product specification (`docs/product-spec.md`, §19) requires that open technical and product decisions are made explicitly, not hidden inside implementation. Record each one here when it is made or changed.

Status values: **Accepted** (in force), **Proposed** (recommended, awaiting confirmation), **Open** (not yet decided), **Superseded**.

| D10 | Money representation | Accepted | Integer minor units as `bigint` + ISO 4217 code; minor-unit exponent from an explicit ISO 4217 table in `lib/money/currencies.ts`, never inferred from `Intl` or locale | Floating point is never used for amounts. |
| D11 | Money formatting | Accepted | `Intl.NumberFormat` with the decimal **string** of the amount (no float conversion), fraction digits forced to the ISO 4217 exponent | Locale and currency are always passed explicitly. |
| D12 | Decimal arithmetic and rounding policy | Accepted | No decimal library: exact `bigint` arithmetic on integer minor units (D10). Quantities have up to 3 decimals (stored in thousandths); percentages up to 3 decimals (stored in thousandths of a percent). Rounding is half away from zero, to the currency's minor unit, at fixed points only: each line's gross (quantity × unit price), each line's percentage discount, and each tax line, computed once per rate on the sum of the lines carrying that rate. The document total is the sum of those rounded parts, so totals always add up on the page. Discounts are per line in V1 | `lib/documents/calc.ts`, tested with 0-, 2- and 3-decimal currencies. Tax-inclusive documents extract tax per rate as gross × rate ÷ (100 + rate). FX and payment-allocation rounding are added with M3/M4 under the same rule. |
| D13 | Catalogue pricing across currencies | Accepted (owner) | Per-currency prices: a product has at most one price per currency. A quote line uses the price in the quote's currency, or the user enters one when there is none. Prices are never converted between currencies | Lines snapshot description, quantity, unit price and tax at the time, so later catalogue edits never change existing documents (spec §7.4). |
| D14 | Tax handling boundaries (V1) | Accepted (owner) | The workspace defines named tax rates (e.g. "VAT 7.5%"); each line has zero or one rate; each document is tax-exclusive or tax-inclusive; totals show one line per rate. MomentumDesk does not decide which tax applies or claim jurisdiction compliance; no tax engine or e-invoicing in V1 | Multiple or compound taxes per line, and jurisdiction engines, are later work (spec §8). |
| D15 | Quote acceptance evidence | Accepted (owner) | Simple acceptance confirmed by an emailed one-time code: on the public page the customer gives their name, receives a 6-digit code at the quote's recipient email (or an email they enter when the quote has none), and confirms. Recorded: name, verified email, time, IP address and user agent, against the exact quote version. Labelled as acceptance, not an electronic signature; e-signature providers are deferred | Needs email configured (D17) in production. Codes are stored hashed, expire after 10 minutes and allow 5 attempts. Rejection needs no code. |
| D16 | Public document links | Accepted (owner) | A random 256-bit token, stored only as a SHA-256 hash, so a link is shown once when created. A link works until staff revoke it or a newer revision of the quote is sent; a superseded link says so and points nowhere else. After the quote's expiry date the page still shows it, but acceptance is closed | Lookups by token run through a narrow database function, so public pages never get general access to tenant data. |
| D17 | Transactional email provider | Accepted | Resend, through its HTTP API (`lib/email/resend.ts`), behind the provider-neutral `EmailSender` | Configured by `RESEND_API_KEY` and `EMAIL_FROM`; the sending domain must be verified in Resend (SPF/DKIM DNS records). Without them, development logs emails and production refuses to send. No SDK dependency. |
| D18 | Background job / queue service | Accepted (owner chose VPS; mechanism proposed by Claude) | A job table in the app's own PostgreSQL, written in the same transaction as the change that causes it (transactional outbox, spec §11), processed by a long-running worker process from the same codebase (`pnpm worker`): `FOR UPDATE SKIP LOCKED` claims, retries with back-off, a dead-letter state after the last attempt, and manual retry. Every job is idempotent by key | No extra vendor. Several workers can run safely side by side. |
| D19 | Payment providers for first pilot markets | Accepted (owner) | Pilots in Nigeria, Ghana, Kenya, South Africa and the UK/US/EU. Three adapters behind one provider-neutral interface: **Paystack** (NG, GH, KE, ZA, CI: cards, bank transfer, USSD, mobile money, M-PESA), **Stripe** (UK, US, EU and other direct-signup countries) and **Bachs** (cards in USD, African mobile money, stablecoins, bank transfer). A payment button is offered only when the workspace's connected provider supports the invoice's currency | Research Nov 2026 (third-party sources; provider sites were not reachable from the build environment): Paystack does not onboard UK/US/EU businesses; Stripe does not sign up businesses in Nigeria, Ghana or Kenya. Each adapter's currencies, webhook signing and amounts must be confirmed against the provider's sandbox before live use (`docs/go-live.md`). |
| D20 | AI provider, data handling and usage limits | Open (M6) | — | Behind a provider-neutral `AIService`. |
| D21 | Route layout | Accepted | `/` marketing, `/app` authenticated workspace, `/q/{token}` and `/i/{token}` public documents | From §13. |
| D22 | Tenant isolation | Accepted | Two independent layers: `tenantDb()` scopes every query in the app, and Postgres row-level security (forced) filters by a transaction-local organization setting. The app connects as a restricted role without `BYPASSRLS`; migrations use a separate owner URL | See `docs/tenant-isolation.md`. Neon's default owner role bypasses RLS, hence the role split. Cost: each tenant query runs in a short transaction (one extra round trip). |
| D23 | Base currency after creation | Accepted | Fixed. The settings service refuses it and a database trigger rejects the change from any path | Spec §5.4: reports must keep their meaning. Rebasing needs a future decision on exchange-rate history. From the Codex workspace-foundations work. |
| D24 | Changing workspace settings | Accepted | Only through `lib/workspaces/settings-service.ts`: permission re-checked inside a serialisable transaction, compare-and-set on `settingsVersion`, audit entry in the same transaction. Better Auth's organization update endpoint is disabled | Design from the Codex workspace-foundations work, implemented on Prisma and Postgres. |
| D25 | Removing customers | Accepted (archive) / Open (erasure) | Customers are archived, never deleted, in V1: hidden from lists, history kept. Contacts can be removed | Permanent deletion and data-subject erasure need a retention policy (spec §9: "define deletion, retention, export and audit policies") before quotes and invoices reference customers. |
| D26 | Leads and pipeline model | Accepted | One `lead` entity moves through the pipeline from enquiry to won or lost; an opportunity is a lead in a later stage. One pipeline per workspace in V1; stages are data with a `kind` (open, won, lost) and default stages are created on first use. Estimated values are minor units with a currency; board totals are per currency | Stages can be renamed or added later without code changes (spec §7.2). Leads link to customers on conversion (D28). |
| D27 | Timeline, notes and tasks | Accepted | Activities (note, call, email, meeting, message) belong to exactly one lead or customer; the timeline merges them with the record's audit history instead of storing events twice. Tasks belong to at most one record. Both inherit the record's permission. Task reminders are shown in the app (tasks page, dashboard) | Emailed or scheduled reminders need the background job service (D18). Wall-clock times are entered and shown in the workspace's timezone. |
| D28 | Duplicates and lead conversion | Accepted | Possible duplicates (same email, same phone ignoring formatting and country prefix, or same name) are shown on lead and customer pages and in the conversion choice, never merged automatically. Converting a lead creates a customer or links the lead to an existing one; the lead keeps its timeline and tasks and records its customer. A lead converts once | Merging records loses or mixes history and needs a person's judgement, so V1 shows the match and lets people link instead. A merge tool can come later if usage shows the need. |
| D29 | CSV import and export | Accepted | Customers and leads, CSV only (UTF-8, header row). Export and import need `data:export` (owner, admin) plus the records' read or write permission. Imports are all-or-nothing after validating every row with the form rules, up to 1,000 rows and 1 MB; errors name the line and column. Exported cells that a spreadsheet would run as formulas get a leading apostrophe, which import removes | No dependency: a small RFC 4180 reader and writer. Import does not check duplicates in V1 (it creates records); duplicates then show on each record (D28). |
| D30 | Invoice numbering | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | An invoice gets its number when it is issued, never as a draft. Numbers come from the workspace sequence (configurable prefix and digits) inside the issuing transaction, so a failed issue leaves no gap; a number is never reused, and a voided invoice keeps its number | Many tax authorities require sequential invoice numbers without gaps. Drafts are identified by their internal id until issued. |
| D31 | Correcting issued invoices | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | An issued invoice never changes (database guard, as for sent quotes). A mistake is corrected by voiding it, which needs `invoices:void` (owner, admin) and a reason, and issuing a new invoice. A voided invoice cannot take payments | Credit notes, which would allow partial corrections, are later work. |
| D32 | Payments and balances (V1) | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | Payments are recorded manually by `payments:record` (owner, admin, finance) in the invoice's currency, against one invoice, with method, date, reference and a note; a payment cannot exceed the outstanding balance (no overpayments or credit balances yet). Outstanding = total − valid allocations, kept by the database in the same transaction. A wrong payment is voided with a reason, never deleted. Status is derived, not stored: draft, issued, viewed, partially paid, paid, overdue, disputed, void | Provider payments (M4) add attempts and allocations through the same model. Overpayments, credits and multi-invoice payments come with M4/M5. |
| D33 | Quote to invoice | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | Only an accepted quote converts, and only once (database unique key). The invoice copies the accepted lines, totals and customer snapshot verbatim, without recalculating; on the draft, only dates, recipient, notes and terms can change, never lines or amounts | Spec §7.5 and acceptance scenario 1. |
| D34 | Base-currency reporting | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | M3 shows money per currency only, never converted. Exchange-rate snapshots and base-currency totals come with receivables reporting (M5), with the rate, source and date recorded and missing rates shown as missing | Spec §5.4–5.5. |
| D35 | Provider accounts | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | Each workspace connects its own provider account with its own API keys; money goes from the customer to the business's account and never through MomentumDesk (spec §6). Secret keys and webhook secrets are encrypted at rest (AES-256-GCM, key in `PROVIDER_SECRETS_KEY`), never shown again and never logged. Connecting needs `providers:manage` (owner, admin) | Platform models (Stripe Connect, Paystack subaccounts) are not used in V1: they would make MomentumDesk part of the money flow. |
| D36 | Provider payments and reconciliation | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | The customer pays from the invoice page through a payment attempt with an internal id and the amount owed at that moment. An invoice is never marked paid from a browser redirect: only a signed webhook or a server-side lookup through the provider's API, then checked for amount, currency and reference, records the payment, once (unique provider reference; webhook events stored by id). Money received above what is owed (e.g. paid twice) is recorded in full and the excess kept unallocated and flagged for refund, never hidden | Refunds and disputes from providers are recorded as provider events now; refund initiation from MomentumDesk follows. Webhooks are processed in the request with idempotency; the job service (D18) is still decided in M5. |
| D37 | Refunds (V1) | Accepted (proposed by Claude, open to owner change) | A refund returns part or all of a payment, either through the provider's API (Paystack, Stripe) or recorded after the business paid the customer back another way (bank transfer, cash; also for Bachs until its refund API is confirmed). It first reduces the payment's unallocated excess, then its allocation to the invoice, which then shows as owed again (void the invoice if the sale is cancelled). The database refuses refunds beyond what is left of the payment. A provider refund that later fails restores the amounts. Refunds need `payments:refund` (owner, admin), a reason, and are audited | Refunds and disputes started from a provider's own dashboard appear in the provider events and are recorded here by staff; automatic import of those, and partial credit notes, are later work. |
| D38 | Exchange rates for base-currency reporting | Accepted (owner: both) | Daily rates from an exchange-rate provider, plus rates entered by owners and admins, which take precedence. Each rate is stored with base and quote currency, rate, source, effective date and who entered or fetched it; reports use the latest rate on or before the date they need and show the rate and source. A missing rate shows the converted value as unavailable; nothing is guessed (spec §5.4–5.5) | Provider: Open Exchange Rates by default (covers NGN, GHS, KES, ZAR and the other pilot currencies; needs an API key, `FX_PROVIDER_KEY`), behind an adapter so another provider can replace it. Rates are fetched by the worker (D18). |
